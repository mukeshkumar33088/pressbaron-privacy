Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Press Baron: Newspaper Tycoon does not collect, transmit or sell any information about you. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

What the app stores

Your newspapers — the save files, settings, and whether you have bought anything — are stored on your own device and nowhere else. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes them permanently, and there is no copy that could be restored for you.

Permissions

  • Network access, used only by Google Play to sell you an item from the in-game store and to restore a purchase you already made. There is no server of ours for the app to contact, because we do not run one.
  • Vibration, for haptic feedback. You can turn it off in Settings.

The app does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your card, your name or your billing address. The app is only told that a purchase completed, and it records that on your device so the item can be applied and restored.

Bug reports and feedback

Settings has a Report a bug and a Feedback button. Tapping one opens the email app on your phone with a message already written. Nothing is sent unless you press send yourself. The draft contains:

  • the app version and your Android version;
  • if a paper is open, its week number, in-game cash and energy;
  • whatever you choose to write.

If you send it, we receive that message and your email address, the same as any email. We use them only to reply and to fix the problem, never for marketing, and we do not share them with anyone. Ask us to delete your messages at any time at the address below.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. It is a business simulation with fictional themes such as bribes, lawsuits and newspaper scandals.

Fiction

Every person, newspaper, company, city and social network in the game is invented. Any resemblance to real people or organisations is coincidental.

Changes

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version adds advertising or online features — the updated policy will be published here and the date above will change before that version is released.
